## What's Included in the Standard Plan

Everything you need to publish a professional business listing on CSVN:

- **Full business profile page** at `https://www.csvn.in/listings/`
- **Direct contact details** — phone, WhatsApp, email, and website displayed on your profile
- **Category listing** in one of 90+ service categories
- **Service list** — up to 10 services displayed on your profile
- **1 catalogue link** — product catalogue, price list, or brochure (external URL)
- **Location and map link** — help buyers locate you
- **Business hours** displayed on your profile
- **Listed badge** on your listing
- **No cap on how many buyers can contact you** through the platform
- **Rotation within the Standard tier** — your listing rotates among Standard-tier listings for fair placement
- **Payment receipt** for your records
- **Listing activation within 4 business days** of payment verification
- **No commissions** — you keep 100% of every deal you close with a buyer

**Important:** CSVN is a visibility platform. Listing or promotional activity does **not** guarantee enquiries, contracts, or orders. We do not promise a specific number of buyer contacts.

## Why ₹999?

We could charge more. We deliberately don't — for three reasons:

**1. Accessibility.** Many small B2B vendors in India can't afford ₹10,000+ annual listing fees on traditional directories. We want everyone to have a shot at being listed.

**2. Simplicity.** One price means you don't have to analyze tiers or guess which plan is right. You just list.

**3. Fair launch.** We're a new network. Our job right now is to build a strong directory — not to maximize revenue. Pricing will evolve as we grow, but launch vendors lock in this rate.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Is there a free trial?**
No — but ₹999 for a full year is less than ₹84 per month. That's cheaper than a single cup of coffee per week.

**Q: Will I get enquiries?**
We can't guarantee enquiries — that depends on buyer demand, your category, and your profile quality. What we provide is a professional listing on the CSVN directory, visible to buyers browsing the platform. Every Standard listing is rotated for fair placement within its tier.

**Q: Do I need GST to list?**
No. Standard plan does not require GST. We'll issue a simple payment receipt. If you have GST, you can provide it and we'll include it in your receipt.

**Q: Can I upgrade to Premium or Featured later?**
Yes — you'll be notified the moment these plans launch, and Standard vendors will get upgrade discounts.

**Q: Do you take a cut of my deals?**
No. CSVN does not charge commission, processing fees, or transaction fees. Whatever you charge your client, you keep.

**Q: How long does setup take?**
Send us your business details. We review them (1–2 business days). After payment verification, your listing goes live within **4 business days**.

**Q: Can I cancel?**
Yes — email us anytime and we'll remove your listing within **4 business days**. Refunds are governed by our [Refund Policy](/refund/).
