## Why ₹999?

We could charge more. We deliberately don't — for three reasons:

**1. Accessibility.** Many small B2B vendors in India can't afford ₹10,000+ annual listing fees on traditional directories. We want everyone to have a shot at being discovered.

**2. Simplicity.** One price means you don't have to analyze tiers or guess which plan is right. You just list.

**3. Fair launch.** We're a new network. Our job right now is to build a great directory — not to maximize revenue. Pricing will evolve as we grow, but launch vendors lock in this rate.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Is there a free trial?**
No — but ₹999 for a full year is less than ₹84 per month. That's cheaper than a single cup of coffee per week.

**Q: What if I don't get any inquiries?**
We can't guarantee inquiries — that depends on buyer demand and your specific category. But every vendor gets the same fair rotation, so you have the same visibility as anyone else in Standard tier.

**Q: Do I need GST to list?**
No. Standard plan does not require GST. We'll issue a simple receipt. If you have GST, you'll get a compliant tax invoice.

**Q: Can I upgrade to Premium or Featured later?**
Yes — you'll be notified the moment these plans launch, and Standard vendors will get upgrade discounts.

**Q: Do you take a cut of my deals?**
No. CSVN does not charge commission, processing fees, or transaction fees. Whatever you charge your client, you keep.

**Q: How long does setup take?**
Send us your business details. We verify them (1–2 business days). After payment, your listing goes live within 24 hours.

**Q: Can I cancel?**
Yes — email us anytime and we'll remove your listing within 24 hours. Refunds are governed by our [Refund Policy](/refund/).
