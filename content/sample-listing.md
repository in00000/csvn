## What You Can Customize

Every field on your listing is yours to fill in. You provide:

| Field | Example |
| :--- | :--- |
| Business name | "Ambekar Pest Control Services" |
| Tagline | "Trusted pest control partner in Pune since 2008" |
| Category | Pest Control |
| City / State | Pune, Maharashtra |
| Phone / WhatsApp | Your business numbers |
| Email / Website | Your contact channels |
| Address | Your office or service area |
| Business hours | Mon–Sat: 9 AM – 7 PM |
| Description | 2–3 paragraphs about your business |
| Services | Up to 10 specific services |
| Catalogues | Links to your PDFs |
| Clients served | Companies you've worked with |
| Social media | Facebook, Instagram, YouTube |
| Map location | Google Maps link to your business |

## What You Cannot Change

To keep the directory trustworthy and consistent:

- **Header design** — same clean look for every vendor
- **Layout structure** — same sections in same order
- **Verified badge** — granted after our review only
- **Buttons and CTAs** — same Call / WhatsApp / Directions layout

This uniformity is what makes CSVN look professional. Every vendor's page feels equally credible — including yours.
