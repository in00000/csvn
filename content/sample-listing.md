## What's Included in Every Listing

Your Standard profile page will include:

### Header Section
- **Business name** prominently displayed
- **"Verified" green badge** (after our verification)
- **Custom tagline** — one-line description of your business
- **Location** — city and state
- **Primary category** — from our 90+ categories
- **Customer rating** (after reviews accumulate)
- **Year established**
- **Direct Call and WhatsApp buttons** — buyers reach you in one tap

### Sticky Navigation
- Quick jump links to About, Services, Catalogues, Clients, Reviews, Contact

### Quick Actions Bar
- **Call Now** — one-tap phone dialing
- **WhatsApp** — opens chat with prefilled message
- **Get Directions** — opens Google Maps to your location

### Main Content Sections

**About Your Business**
A 2–3 paragraph description of what you do, your experience, and what makes you different.

**Products & Services**
A clean checklist of the specific services you provide. Up to 10 services displayed.

**Catalogues**
Direct links to your product catalogues, price lists, or brochures as PDFs. Buyers can click to view or download.

**Clients We Serve**
A showcase of your marquee clients — badges showing the well-known companies you've worked with. Builds instant credibility.

**Customer Reviews**
Individual reviews from verified customers, with names, star ratings, and their written feedback.

**Contact Information**
A full grid with:
- Address
- Phone (clickable)
- Email (clickable)
- Website (opens in new tab)
- Working hours
- WhatsApp chat button

### Sidebar Widgets

**Quick Information**
Category, location, experience, established year, rating, and status — all in a compact card.

**Catalogues**
Quick-access links to all your catalogues.

**Connect With Us**
Buttons to Call, WhatsApp, Email, Website, and social media (Facebook, Instagram, YouTube).

**Location**
A clickable map widget that opens Google Maps.

**Need Help?**
A "Contact Business" button for buyers ready to reach out.

**Report an Issue**
A link for buyers to report incorrect information — protects your reputation too.

## What You Can Customize

Every field on your listing is customisable. You provide:

| Field | Example |
| :--- | :--- |
| Business name | "Ambekar Pest Control Services" |
| Tagline | "Trusted pest control partner in Pune since 2008" |
| Category | Pest Control |
| City / State | Pune, Maharashtra |
| Phone / WhatsApp | Your business numbers |
| Email / Website | Your contact channels |
| Address | Your office or service area |
| Business hours | Mon–Sat: 9 AM – 7 PM |
| Description | 2–3 paragraphs about your business |
| Services | Up to 10 specific services |
| Catalogues | Links to your PDFs |
| Clients served | Names of companies you've worked with |
| Social media | Facebook, Instagram, YouTube |
| Map location | Google Maps link to your business |

## What You Cannot Change

To keep the directory trustworthy and consistent:

- **Header design** — same clean look for every vendor
- **Layout structure** — same sections in same order
- **Verified badge criteria** — granted after our review only
- **Buttons and CTAs** — same Call/WhatsApp/Directions layout

This uniformity is what makes CSVN look professional — every vendor's page feels equally credible.
