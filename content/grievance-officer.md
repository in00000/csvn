## What You Can File a Grievance About

The Grievance Officer handles complaints related to:

- Inaccurate or misleading vendor listings
- Content that violates our Terms of Service
- Copyright or intellectual property violations
- Privacy violations or misuse of personal information
- Fraudulent vendor activity
- Harassment or abuse by any user
- Any other violation of IT Rules, 2021

## How to File a Complaint

Email your complaint to [grievance@csvn.in](mailto:grievance@csvn.in) with:

1. **Your full name** and contact details
2. **Description** of the issue
3. **URL(s)** of the specific content or listing
4. **Evidence** (screenshots, documents, if applicable)
5. **Expected resolution** — what you want us to do
6. **Declaration** that the information provided is accurate

## What Happens After You File

1. **Acknowledgment** — You receive an email confirming receipt within 24 hours.
2. **Investigation** — We review the complaint, and contact relevant parties if needed.
3. **Decision** — We determine the appropriate action (remove content, warn vendor, suspend listing, etc.).
4. **Resolution** — You receive a written decision within 15 days.
5. **Appeal** — If you disagree with the outcome, you can escalate to the **Grievance Appellate Committee** (gac.gov.in) as per IT Rules, 2021.

## For Complaints About Specific Vendors

If your complaint is about a specific vendor's conduct, please note:

- CSVN is a **directory platform** and is not a party to any transaction between you and a vendor.
- We can remove a listing if it violates our Terms, but we cannot mediate commercial disputes.
- For commercial disputes, contact the vendor directly or approach consumer forums.

## Data Protection Complaints

For complaints about how your personal data is handled, you may also contact:

- **CSVN privacy contact:** [privacy@csvn.in](mailto:privacy@csvn.in)
- **Data Protection Board of India** — under the DPDP Act, 2023

## Report Incorrect Information

For routine corrections (wrong phone number, outdated address, etc.), email [report@csvn.in](mailto:report@csvn.in). These are typically resolved faster than formal grievances.
