## Fair Rotation & Network Guarantee

**Auto-rotation policy.** To ensure fairness and consistent visibility for all featured partners, Pro and VIP listings are powered by an automated priority rotation system across the homepage, category pages, and monthly email digests. No listing is permanently buried.

- **Top 10 Pool (Featured Pro)** — your listing rotates within the top 10 positions of your category page.
- **Top 3 Pool (VIP Leader)** — your listing rotates within the top 3 positions of your category page and is included in the homepage featured slider rotation.

**Direct connection, zero commission.** CSVN is a direct media reach network. We charge **zero commission** on closed deals. Every enquiry connects directly to your business phone, WhatsApp, or website — we never sit in the middle of your transaction.

## About the HR & Decision-Maker Digest

The CSVN HR & Decision-Maker Digest is a monthly email sent to our subscriber list of HR Heads, Admin Heads, Procurement Heads, Purchase Heads, and other corporate decision-makers across India.

- **Featured Pro** vendors receive **24 monthly mentions** (one per month across the 2-year term).
- **VIP Leader** vendors receive an exclusive **“Top Partner Spotlight”** placement in all 24 monthly digests.

Digest inclusion is a promotional placement. It does not guarantee opens, clicks, or enquiries.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Is there a free trial?**
No. The Starter plan is ₹999 for a full year — less than ₹84 per month. If you want a longer runway and stronger placement, Featured Pro works out to ~₹208 per month over two years.

**Q: Will I get enquiries?**
We can’t guarantee enquiries — that depends on buyer demand, your category, and your profile quality. What we provide is a professional listing on the CSVN directory, visible to buyers browsing the platform, plus rotation and promotional placement depending on your plan. Every listing rotates for fair placement within its pool.

**Q: What’s the difference between the rotation pools?**
Starter listings rotate across all Standard-tier listings in a category. Featured Pro listings rotate within the **Top 10** positions of the category page. VIP Leader listings rotate within the **Top 3** positions of the category page and are also included in the homepage featured slider.

**Q: Do I need GST to list?**
No. No plan requires GST. We’ll issue a simple payment receipt. If you have GST, you can provide it and we’ll include it in your receipt.

**Q: Can I upgrade later?**
Yes — you can upgrade from Starter to Featured Pro or VIP Leader at any time by paying the difference. See [Upgrading Your Plan](#upgrading-your-plan) above.

**Q: Do you take a cut of my deals?**
No. CSVN does not charge commission, processing fees, or transaction fees on any plan. Whatever you charge your client, you keep.

**Q: How long does setup take?**
Send us your business details. We review them (1–2 business days). After payment verification, your listing goes live within **4 business days**. If you’re upgrading, the new placement and rotation benefits apply within the same window.

**Q: How do I pay ₹4,999 or ₹9,999?**
UPI works for all amounts. For Featured Pro and VIP Leader, bank transfer (NEFT/IMPS) is also available on request — email us and we’ll send you the account details.

**Q: Can I cancel?**
Yes — email us anytime and we’ll remove your listing within **4 business days**. Refunds are governed by our [Refund Policy](/refund/).

## How to Get Started

**Step 1 — Choose your plan.** Starter (₹999 / 1 year), Featured Pro (₹4,999 / 2 years), or VIP Leader (₹9,999 / 2 years).

**Step 2 — Create your listing.** Use our [payment and listing form](/payment/) to submit your business details, or email [info@csvn.in](mailto:info@csvn.in) with:

- Business name (exactly as you want it displayed)
- City and state
- Primary category (from our [90+ categories](/categories/))
- Phone, WhatsApp, and email
- 2–3 sentence description of your services
- List of 5–10 specific services you offer
- Website URL (if you have one)
- Catalogue / price list / brochure link (Featured Pro and VIP Leader)

**Step 3 — We review.** 1–2 business days. We review your listing details to make sure they are complete and match our listing guidelines.

**Step 4 — You pay.** Pay by UPI on the [payment page](/payment/), or ask us for a payment link. Bank transfer is available on request for Featured Pro and VIP Leader.

**Step 5 — Your listing goes live.** Within **4 business days** of payment verification. VIP Leader homepage rotation begins in the same window.
