## How to List — Step by Step

### Step 1: Choose Your Plan

Pick the plan that matches your visibility goals:

- **Starter** — ₹999 / 1 year
- **Featured Pro** — ₹4,999 / 2 years
- **VIP Leader** — ₹9,999 / 2 years

### Step 2: Send Us Your Business Details

Email [info@csvn.in](mailto:info@csvn.in?subject=CSVN%20Listing%20Enquiry) with:

- **Business name** (exactly as you want it displayed)
- **Primary category** (choose from our [90+ categories](/categories/))
- **City and state** of operation
- **Contact details** — phone, WhatsApp, email
- **Website URL** (if you have one)
- **Service description** — 2 to 3 sentences about what you offer
- **List of key services** — 5–10 specific services you provide
- **Catalogue / price list / brochure link** (Featured Pro and VIP Leader)

### Step 3: We Review Your Listing

We review your submission to check:

- Listing details are complete
- Service category is accurate
- Contact information is valid

**Typical review time: 1–2 business days.**

### Step 4: Pay for Your Plan

Pay by UPI on our [payment page](/payment/), or ask us for a payment link.

- **Starter:** ₹999
- **Featured Pro:** ₹4,999
- **VIP Leader:** ₹9,999

Bank transfer (NEFT/IMPS) is available on request for Featured Pro and VIP Leader.

### Step 5: Your Listing Goes Live

Once reviewed and paid, your listing goes live within **4 business days** of payment verification.

You’ll receive:

- A confirmation email with your listing URL
- A payment receipt (and a GST-inclusive receipt if you provide your GSTIN)
- Instructions for requesting edits in the future

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Why do plans start at ₹999?**
The Starter plan is designed to make CSVN accessible to every business. Featured Pro and VIP Leader are for vendors who want stronger placement, longer validity, and promotional reach. [See full pricing →](/pricing/)

**Q: Do you take a commission on deals?**
No. CSVN connects you directly with buyers. All commercial terms are between you and the buyer. We charge zero commission on closed deals.

**Q: How do buyers contact me?**
Through the phone, WhatsApp, email, and website links on your listing page. Buyers reach out to you directly.

**Q: How long does the review take?**
Typically 1–2 business days.

**Q: Will I get enquiries?**
We can’t guarantee enquiries — that depends on buyer demand, your category, and your profile. What we provide is a professional listing on the CSVN directory, visible to decision-makers browsing the platform. Every listing gets rotation within its tier or pool for fair placement.

**Q: What is the difference between the rotation pools?**
Starter listings rotate across Standard-tier listings in a category. Featured Pro listings rotate within the **Top 10** positions of the category page. VIP Leader listings rotate within the **Top 3** positions and are also included in the homepage featured slider.

**Q: Do I need GST to list?**
No. No plan requires GST. We’ll issue a simple payment receipt. If you have GST, provide it and we’ll include it in your receipt.

**Q: Can I upgrade later?**
Yes — you can upgrade from Starter to Featured Pro or VIP Leader at any time by paying the difference. Email us with your business name and the plan you want to upgrade to.

**Q: What if I want to remove my listing?**
Email [info@csvn.in](mailto:info@csvn.in) and we’ll remove it within **4 business days**. No questions asked.

**Q: Can I cancel?**
Yes — email us anytime. Refunds are governed by our [Refund Policy](/refund/).

**Q: How do I pay?**
We accept UPI for all plans. Bank transfer (NEFT/IMPS) is available on request for Featured Pro and VIP Leader. You can pay directly through our [payment page](/payment/). If you need an alternative method, contact us.
