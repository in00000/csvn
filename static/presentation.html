Which Is Right for You
Decision Guide

Which option is right for you?

Two very different products. Both are valid choices — depending on your business stage and budget.

Choose a Lead Generation Directory if...

You have a large budget (₹15,000+ per year), need aggressive lead flow, and can handle high volumes of enquiries. You are looking for maximum volume, and you are willing to pay for it.

Choose CSVN if...

You want a professional corporate listing, direct buyer contact, no commissions, fair rotation, and a price that does not strain your business. You want visibility without gambling ₹10,000+ per year.

You can also use both together

Many vendors use CSVN as a low-cost foundation and add paid directories when their business volume supports it. The two are not mutually exclusive. CSVN at ₹999 is a low-risk way to establish a corporate listing.

Important: "Lead Generation Directory" is used here as a general category descriptor. It does not refer to any specific company, brand, or platform. Pricing, features, and policies vary widely.
