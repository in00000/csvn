Why vendors choose CSVN

Three structural differences that make CSVN cheaper, faster, and more transparent for corporate service providers.

Priced for small vendors

₹999 per year. Not ₹10,000, not ₹25,000. Because most corporate service providers in India cannot afford traditional directory fees.

No commission, ever

Whatever you charge your client, you keep 100%. We are not a party to any deal between you and a buyer.

Honest about outcomes

We do not promise leads. We promise visibility. If it does not work for you, you simply do not renew. No lock-in.

Which is right for you?

  • A lead generation directory makes sense if you have a large budget (₹15,000+ per year), need aggressive lead flow, and can handle high volumes of enquiries.
  • CSVN makes sense if you want a professional corporate listing, direct buyer contact, no commissions, and a price that does not strain your business.
  • Both together also work. Many vendors use CSVN as a low-cost foundation and add paid directories when their business volume supports it.

Our honest position

We are not trying to beat traditional directories on scale. We are offering an affordable, transparent alternative for vendors who cannot justify ₹10,000+ per year. If you need aggressive lead volume and have the budget, a larger platform may serve you better. If you want a professional corporate listing at a fair price, CSVN is built for you.
